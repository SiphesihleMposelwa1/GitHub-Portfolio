Reflection

WRITE 150-300 words in your own words. Cover:
  1. What: how you planned sprints (four cards each, with dates and checklists).
  2. Why it mattered: how short sprints and checklists kept the team on track.
  3. Learned: planned versus completed with numbers (e.g. Sprint 8's Develop Home Page at 4/5, or Sprint 10's overdue dates).
  4. Challenge: a delay or blocker and how you handled it.
  5. Do differently: how you would estimate and plan the next sprints.
  6. Connection: how sprints came from the product backlog and are recorded in the logbook.
