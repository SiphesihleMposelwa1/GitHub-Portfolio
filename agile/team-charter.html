Reflection

WRITE 150-300 words in your own words. Cover:
  1. What: During Sprint 1, our team created the BrightStart Agile Team Charter to define the project's purpose, team roles, responsibilities, and working agreements. Our project aims to make English reading, writing, and comprehension practice engaging and accessible for learners in Grades 1–3. Sabotseng Ndaba was the Product Owner, Siphesihle Mposelwa was the Agile Lead, Lindokuhle Nanto was the UI/UX Designer, and Mhlana Sisonke was the Developer/Tester.
  2. Why it mattered: The charter helped the team understand individual responsibilities and established guidelines for communication, task management, and collaboration. This supported a shared understanding of our project goals..
  3. Learned: I learned that clear communication and defined responsibilities are important for avoiding misunderstandings and duplicated work. Working agreements help team members understand what is expected of them..
  4. Challenge: As Agile Lead, I may need to address missed deadlines, communication problems, or tasks that are not progressing as planned. I would encourage team members to raise problems early, discuss solutions respectfully, and agree on the next steps..
  5. Do differently: In future projects, I would review the charter regularly with the team to ensure that our working agreements remain relevant and that everyone understands their responsibilities..
  6. Connection: The charter provides guidance for sprint planning, task allocation, and logbook updates. It helps the team monitor progress, record challenges, and make decisions that support the BrightStart project's goals..
Starter: "When we defined our team roles in Sprint 1, I realised that giving each team member clear responsibilities helps us understand what we need to do, avoid duplicated work, and work together towards achieving the BrightStart project's goals."
