About this portfolio

This e-portfolio documents how my team applied Agile practices to plan and build the BrightStart English Tutorial Service, a community website that offers English tutoring with registration, quizzes and progress tracking. It follows the four e-portfolio steps: Collect artefacts, Select the best evidence, Reflect on what I learned, and Connect the pieces into one story.

Our work is managed on a Trello board with 15 sprints, from project set-up in June through to final testing and presentation: BrightStart English Tutorial Service board. The code lives in our GitHub repository: [add your repository link].

I served as the Agile Lead for this project. My contribution involved helping the team organise tasks, follow our working agreements, and use Trello to monitor progress. Through this assignment, I reflected on how Agile practices supported our teamwork, planning, and development of the BrightStart website.

What I Learned:

Through the BrightStart English Tutorial Service project, I learned how the five Agile artefacts worked together as one workflow, from planning the project to developing the website and reflecting on our progress.

  • Team Charter and Roles (Sprint 1): The team charter helped us understand the project's purpose, define our roles, and establish working agreements. As the Agile Lead, I learned that clear responsibilities and communication help the team work together towards a common goal.
  • Business Idea and User Stories (Sprints 2–6): The business idea, survey, and system requirements helped us identify the needs of learners, parents, and teachers. We used these needs to develop user stories and organise the required features into the product backlog.
  • Product Backlog and Sprint Planning (Sprint 7): Prioritising the backlog helped the team identify important features and divide the work into manageable tasks. This guided the development of the BrightStart website during Sprints 8–10.
  • Agile Logbook and Retrospectives: The logbook helped us record our progress, challenges, and activities, while retrospectives encouraged us to consider what worked well and what could be improved. I learned that Agile is not only about completing tasks but also about communicating, adapting to challenges, and improving the way the team works throughout a project.
