About this portfolio

This e-portfolio documents how my team applied Agile practices to plan and build the BrightStart English Tutorial Service, a community website that offers English tutoring with registration, quizzes and progress tracking. It follows the four e-portfolio steps: Collect artefacts, Select the best evidence, Reflect on what I learned, and Connect the pieces into one story.
