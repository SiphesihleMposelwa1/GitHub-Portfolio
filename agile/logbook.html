Reflection

WRITE 150-300 words in your own words. Cover:
  1. What: how and when you recorded planning, stand-ups and the retrospective.
  2. Why it mattered: how the logbook kept you accountable.
  3. Learned: one blocker you logged and how it was resolved.
  4. Challenge: staying consistent with entries.
  5. Do differently: how you would use retrospectives to change the next sprint.
  6. Connection: how the logbook tracked the sprint backlog and fed back into the team charter.
